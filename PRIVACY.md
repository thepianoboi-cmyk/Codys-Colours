# Privacy

Codys Colours runs locally inside Adobe Illustrator and Adobe Photoshop.

- The panel does not send your colour libraries to a server.
- Imported CSV and JSON libraries are stored locally in the panel's browser storage.
- Palette, favourite, recent, and pattern history are stored locally.
- The installer copies files into Adobe extension and script folders on your Mac.

If you share screenshots, exported palettes, or imported library files yourself, that sharing is outside the plugin's local storage.
